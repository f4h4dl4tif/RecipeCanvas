RecipeCanvas

RecipeCanvas is a recipe and cooking-experiment studio: build personal recipes, save versions as you tweak them, compare versions, log substitutions, cook step by step with timers and print recipe cards.

Your recipes stay on this Mac. No account, no tracking, no paid tier.

This installer puts RecipeCanvas in your Applications folder and opens it when it's done.

Requires macOS 14 Sonoma or later on a Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 or newer).
